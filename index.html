Use It Up – Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Use It Up is an Android app that helps you keep track of the food you have, reminds you before it expires and suggests recipes that use it. It is made by an independent developer, Ehud Suryano ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

In short: the app has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your food list, shopping list, meal plan, recipes and settings stay on your phone. A few features need the internet, and the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Both are described below.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your food items, quantities and dates, shopping list, meal plan, saved recipes, usage statistics and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification goes through a server.
  • The home-screen widget reads the same on-device data.
  • If you export a backup, the file is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to share or save it.

We never receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it, unless you kept a backup.

Features that use the internet

  • Web recipe search. To find recipes, the app sends the names of your foods (without dates or quantities), or the words you type, to the DuckDuckGo search engine. It then loads the recipe web pages from the search results. Those websites receive the usual information any website receives, such as your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • In-app browser and "Save from a link". When you open or save a recipe page, the app loads it from that website. The app's optional ad blocking only affects these third-party pages.
  • Barcode scanning. The camera is used only while you scan, to read the barcode. No photo or video is saved or sent. The barcode number alone is sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product name.

All other features work offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. The app asks Google for non-personalised ads only. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information about ads.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and ads load only if your choices allow them. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • We don't collect your location, contacts, microphone audio or files.
  • We don't sell your information, and we don't receive the data described above.

Permissions

  • Camera: only for barcode scanning, while you scan.
  • Notifications: for expiry reminders.
  • Internet: for web recipes, barcode lookups and ads.
  • Storage (Android 12 and older only): to read or save a backup file that you pick.

Children

Use It Up is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security and retention

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your phone's own security. We don't keep any copy. Information sent to DuckDuckGo, Open Food Facts, recipe websites or Google is kept according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
